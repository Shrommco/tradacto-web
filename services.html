Core Pillar 01

Heavy Electrical Technical Auditing

Tradacto deploys independent Swedish electrical engineers to conduct rigorous pre-qualification audits, material traceability checks, and full Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT) directly on foundry floors in Asia.

Commanded by a 20-year VLCC Master Mariner, our marine logistics engine engineers every phase of transit—eliminating port-draft bottlenecks, securing geared heavy-lift vessel charters, and insulating cargo underwritten by premier marine underwriters.

Core Pillar 03

Foundry Slot Origination & Trade Finance

We eliminate counterparty and currency risk through institutional trade finance structuring. By utilizing back-to-back, UCP 600 compliant Letters of Credit (LCs), we secure production slots without exposing client capital to foreign exchange volatility or insolvency risk.

Our dual-entity structure (Swedish Holding Company + Maritime Operations) captures a clean net spread per transformer unit upon verified delivery while shielding U.S. buyers from direct cross-border friction.

  • Financial Instrument UCP 600 Transferable & Back-to-Back LCs
  • Regulatory Framework Swedish Corporate Jurisdiction
  • Spread Capture Optimized $3M–$4M Unit Arbitrage
